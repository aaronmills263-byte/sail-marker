# Sail Marker — Outreach Runbook

## Weekly Target

- **25 new prospects** added to the prospect list per week
- **15 cold emails** sent per week (personalised, not bulk)
- Split across tiers: 5 charter/marina, 5 school/broker, 5 services/sponsorship

## Expected Metrics

| Stage | Benchmark |
|-------|-----------|
| Open rate | 40-55% |
| Reply rate | 8-15% |
| Positive reply rate | 4-8% |
| Close rate (of positive replies) | 30-50% |
| Expected new partners/month | 3-6 |

## When Someone Replies Positively

1. Reply within 2 hours (same business day minimum)
2. Send a Loom or screenshot showing exactly where their listing would appear on the destination page
3. Offer a 15-minute call — use this link: [CAL_LINK]
4. On the call: confirm tier, confirm which destination/region pages, walk through the partner dashboard
5. Send Stripe payment link within 1 hour of call
6. Onboard within 48 hours of payment (collect logo, blurb, fleet details, link URL)

## Discount Playbook

Use only when the prospect pushes back on price or goes silent after a positive reply.

| Scenario | Offer |
|----------|-------|
| First objection on price | 50% off month one |
| Wants to "try it first" | 14-day free trial (standard) |
| Multi-base / multi-region | 25% off first three months |
| Asks for annual pricing | 2 months free on annual commitment (10 months for the price of 12) |
| Competitor is cheaper | Match + add one extra destination page placement |

Never discount below 50% of list price. Never offer free months beyond month one.

## Seasonal Notes

- **Charter booking season peaks January-March** — ramp up charter partner outreach in November-December
- Marina and provisioning services convert best in spring (April-May) before sailing season
- Sailing school outreach works year-round but peaks before summer and Caribbean winter season
- Yacht broker outreach aligns with boat show calendar (September-January)

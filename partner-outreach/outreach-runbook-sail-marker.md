# Sail Marker -- Outreach Runbook

Operational guide for partner outreach. Keep this document updated as processes evolve.

---

## 1. Weekly Prospect Target

- **15-20 new prospects per week.** This is a smaller niche -- be selective rather than high-volume. Quality of fit matters more than quantity.
- Split across tiers based on current pipeline gaps. Prioritise Charter Partner and Marina Listing prospects as these are the highest-value tiers.

---

## 2. Expected Metrics

| Metric                        | Benchmark                  |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Reply rate                    | 3-5%                       |
| Closes per 100 emails sent    | 1-2                        |
| Note                          | Marine industry may skew slightly higher due to smaller community and stronger word-of-mouth effects. Track and adjust benchmarks quarterly. |

---

## 3. Price Pushback Playbook

Use sequentially when a prospect objects to pricing. Do not lead with discounts -- only offer when there is genuine resistance.

| Stage              | Offer                                    | Detail                                                    |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| First objection    | 50% off month 1                          | Half-price trial month to demonstrate value before full commitment |
| Second objection   | 25% off first 3 months                   | Extended discount runway; positions the partnership as low-risk |
| Walk-away floor    | 50% of list price                        | Absolute minimum. Do not go below this under any circumstances |

**Tier-specific floors:**

| Tier                       | List Price  | Walk-Away Floor |
|----------------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Charter Partner            | $500/mo     | $250/mo         |
| Sailing School             | $200/mo     | $100/mo         |
| Yacht Broker               | $300/mo     | $150/mo         |
| Marina Listing             | $150/mo     | $75/mo          |
| Provisioning & Services    | $100/mo     | $50/mo          |
| Regional Sponsorship       | From $1,500/mo | $750/mo      |

## 4. Handling Positive Replies

1. Respond within the same business day -- ideally within 2 hours.
2. Send the Cal.com booking link: **[INSERT_CAL_LINK]**
3. Schedule a **15-minute call within 48 hours** of the positive reply.
4. On the call: confirm the tier, walk through the destination page placement, answer questions, and agree on next steps.
5. Send a Stripe payment link within 1 hour of call completion.
6. Onboard within 48 hours of payment (collect logo, listing copy, fleet details, destination preferences).

## 5. One-Week Rhythm Template

**Important:** Align outreach timing with charter booking seasons. January through March is peak booking for summer Mediterranean charters. September through November is peak for winter Caribbean. Front-load outreach to these segments in the months before their respective windows.

| Day       | Activity                                                                                      |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Source 5-7 new prospects. Send initial outreach emails (Charter Partner and Yacht Broker tiers). Review any weekend replies. |
| Tuesday   | Follow up on previous week's opens and non-replies (first follow-up). Research prospect backgrounds for Wednesday sends. |
| Wednesday | Source 5-7 new prospects. Send initial outreach emails (Sailing School and Marina Listing tiers). |
| Thursday  | Follow up on all outstanding threads. Schedule calls for Friday. Prepare any custom pitch materials for confirmed calls. |
| Friday    | Send initial outreach emails (Provisioning & Services tier). Conduct all scheduled prospect calls. Update prospect list statuses and log notes. |
